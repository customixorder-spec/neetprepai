SECTION I: PHYSICS (Questions 1 to 45 • 180 Marks)
Q1. Physics - Kinematics & Motion
A projectile is fired at an angle of 45° with the horizontal with initial velocity u. What is the ratio of its maximum height H to its horizontal range R?
(1) 1 : 4
(2) 1 : 2
(3) 1 : 1
(4) 4 : 1
NCERT Solution: H = u^2 sin^2(45) / (2g) = u^2 / (4g). R = u^2 sin(90) / g = u^2 / g. H / R = 1 / 4. Correct Option: (1).
Q2. Physics - Current Electricity & Wheatstone Bridge
In a Wheatstone bridge circuit, arms P = 10 Ω, Q = 30 Ω, R = 15 Ω, S = 45 Ω. What is the current flowing through the central galvanometer?
(1) Zero (Bridge is balanced)
(2) 1.5 Amperes
(3) 0.5 Amperes
(4) Maximum possible current
NCERT Solution: P/Q = 10/30 = 1/3. R/S = 15/45 = 1/3. Since P/Q = R/S, the bridge is balanced and no current flows through galvanometer. Correct Option: (1).

SECTION II: CHEMISTRY (Questions 46 to 90 • 180 Marks)
Q46. Chemistry - Organic Reactions (Reimer-Tiemann)
Phenol reacts with chloroform (CHCl3) and aqueous NaOH followed by acidification to produce which major organic compound?
(1) Salicylaldehyde
(2) Salicylic acid
(3) Benzoic acid
(4) Anisole
NCERT Solution: Reimer-Tiemann reaction introduces -CHO group at ortho position of phenol yielding Salicylaldehyde as major product. Correct Option: (1).

Q91. Botany - Plant Physiology (Photosynthesis)
What is the primary electron acceptor for electrons excited from Photosystem II (P680) in non-cyclic photophosphorylation?
(1) Pheophytin
(2) Plastocyanin
(3) Ferredoxin
(4) Plastoquinone
NCERT Solution: NCERT Biology Page 212: Pheophytin accepts electrons excited from PS II (P680) and transfers them to plastoquinone. Correct Option: (1).

Q136. Zoology - Human Physiology & Hormones
Which hormone secreted by G-cells of the stomach mucosa stimulates gastric acid (HCl) and pepsinogen secretion?
(1) Gastrin
(2) Secretin
(3) Cholecystokinin
(4) GIP
NCERT Solution: Gastrin produced by G-cells acts on parietal cells to stimulate HCl secretion. Correct Option: (1).
